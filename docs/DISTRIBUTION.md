# Pujo Guide: distribution plan for Durga Puja 2026

Written 15 Oct 2026 (Panchami/Shashthi eve). The window is short: Shashthi 16/17 Oct → Bijoya 21–22 Oct, with
greetings traffic running to about 31 Oct. Everything here is free and needs one person with a phone.

## What to send, by day (the "hook" changes daily)

| Day | What people want to share | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Panchami–Shashthi | "Which Puja character are you?" cards, squad role cards | `/bn/play/puja-personality`, `/bn/play/puja-squad` |
| Every morning | **শুভ ষষ্ঠী / সপ্তমী / অষ্টমী…** cards with the family's name | `/bn/greetings` (today's card is auto-picked) |
| Every evening | "এখন কেমন ভিড়?" live board, tonight's route | `/bn/local`, `/bn/puja/kolkata`, `/bn/puja/dhaka` |
| Navami–Dashami | Passport card ("৭টা প্যান্ডেল ঘোরা হল!") | `/bn/passport` |
| Dashami–Bijoya | **শুভ বিজয়া** cards (the biggest single day of sharing) | `/bn/greetings?d=bijoya` |

Always send the Bangla URL to Bangla groups and the `/en/` URL to diaspora groups.

## Channels, in order of expected payoff

1. **WhatsApp family and para groups (highest).** Send the greeting card image + link each morning at 7–8 am.
   Ask three people to forward to their groups. The card carries the site name, so every forward is an ad.
2. **WhatsApp Status / Instagram Story.** Post the story-size card (1080×1920) daily with the link sticker.
3. **Facebook groups.** Kolkata: "Kolkata Durga Puja", "Durga Puja Pandal Hopping", para/club pages. Dhaka:
   "Dhaka Puja", "Sanatan Dharma Bangladesh" style community groups. Diaspora: Bengali association groups
   (London BCSC, Hounslow, Toronto Kalibari, Bongosathi Melbourne, BAS Singapore, Houston, Atlanta,
   Bay Area). Post the greeting card, not a link alone; links get buried, cards get saved and forwarded.
4. **Puja committees (Kolkata and Dhaka).** Message the committee page of every pandal in the directory:
   "Your puja is listed with sources; visitors can report crowds live." Ask them to share their own page
   (`/bn/puja/<id>`) and, if possible, print a small "এখন কেমন ভিড়? → pujoguide.vercel.app" sign at the queue.
5. **Diaspora associations.** The abroad page (`/en/puja/abroad`) lists them; email each one their own entry
   and ask them to share it in their newsletter. They love being listed.
6. **Reddit.** r/kolkata, r/bangladesh, r/india (Puja megathreads), r/hinduism. One honest post: what it is,
   that it's free and sourced, and a screenshot of the crowd board. Reply to every comment.
7. **Reels/Shorts.** Use the site's own "Reel idea" generator: film one shot a day, caption from the caption
   generator, add the greeting card as the last frame, hashtags #ShubhoAshtami #DurgaPuja2026 #PujoGuide.
8. **Press.** The Telegraph (My Kolkata), Anandabazar digital, Daily Star (Dhaka) and bdnews24 run Puja tech
   stories every year. A two-line pitch: "A free, sourced Puja guide where visitors report crowds live, in
   Bangla and English; built by one person." Include two screenshots.
9. **Search.** Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console today; the dates page and the Kolkata/Dhaka
   pages are built for "durga puja 2026 date", "kolkata pandal 2026", "শুভ বিজয়া শুভেচ্ছা" searches.
   Results compound over days, so earlier is better.

## Rules that keep it welcome, not spammy

- One post per group per day, always with something new (today's card, tonight's board).
- Never post the same text twice; the cards do the talking.
- Reply fast in the first hour; algorithms reward early comments.
- Fix anything a user reports the same day and say so publicly.
- Don't claim crowd data is official; the site already labels it as visitor reports.

## Measure

Google Analytics (`NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID`): page views by `/bn` vs `/en`, `share` events by method, `game_result`,
`save_image`, `live_report`, `greeting_line`. Check Realtime during the evenings. Whatever channel brings the
most `share` events the first night is the one to double down on.
